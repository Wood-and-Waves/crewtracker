# Overview video — shooting list

Click by click, in **recording order**, for a silent screen capture that the
narration goes over afterwards. The script is the companion file; this is only
what your hands do.

Three of the nine takes are on a phone. Two of them are on a **different show**
from the rest, and that is the thing most likely to trip you up — see "Which
show" below.

---

## Two things to settle BEFORE you record

**1. Two roles in the script are not in the demo directory.**
"Stage manager" and "operators" have nobody holding them, so the fill picker in
§3 would open on *"Nobody in the directory is listed as Stage Manager"* — which
looks like the app is broken when it is doing exactly what it should.

What the directory actually holds:

| Role | People |
|---|---|
| BO Tech | 6 — Desmond, Farrah, Gil, Hana, Lucia, Owen |
| Stagehand | 4 — Dana, Miles, Nina, Ruth |
| A1 | 3 — Alex, Bianca, Chris |
| LD | 3 — Marcus, Rosa, Tobias |
| V1 | 3 — Elias, Jordan, Sam |
| Camera Operator | 2 — Sofia, Theo |
| A2 | 1 — Priya |

Easiest fix is two words in the narration: **"stagehand"** instead of stage
manager, **"BO techs"** instead of operators. Say the word and I will add the
other two roles to the directory instead, and your script stays as written.

**2. Unlock Meridian Partner Summit.** It is closed out, so §5's *Send Final
Report* reads "Final report sent" and cannot be pressed. Unlock it first and the
press is available — and pressing it at the end of §5 re-locks it, which is the
shot. Check **Settings → Final Report recipients** is your own address first,
because that press sends a real email.

## If something surfaces

Two things are true of the demo and worth knowing before you blame the app:

- **Dates move on every reseed.** The show you build is Oct 1–5 because you type
  it, but Lakeshore's overlap is computed from the same anchor — so check the
  picker before recording rather than trusting these notes.
- **Every email in this company comes to you.** That is the point, but it means
  the inbox fills up during a session. Clear it before take 3 so the right
  message is the top one.
