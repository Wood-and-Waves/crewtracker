h1>Overview video — shooting list

Click by click, in recording order, for a silent screen capture that the narration goes over afterwards. The script is the companion file; this is only what your hands do.

Three of the nine takes are on a phone. Two of them are on a different show from the rest, and that is the thing most likely to trip you up — see "Which show" below.

2. Unlock Meridian Partner Summit. It is closed out, so §5's *Send Final Report* reads "Final report sent" and cannot be pressed. Unlock it first and the press is available — and pressing it at the end of §5 re-locks it, which is the shot. If something surfaces

Two things are true of the demo and worth knowing before you blame the app:

  • Dates move on every reseed. The show you build is Oct 1–5 because you type it, but Lakeshore's overlap is computed from the same anchor — so check the picker before recording rather than trusting these notes.
  • Every email in this company comes to you. That is the point, but it means the inbox fills up during a session. Clear it before take 3 so the right message is the top one.
